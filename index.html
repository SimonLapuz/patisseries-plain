About us

Company background

Pâtisseries Plain was inspired by Ladurée, the Parisian bakery founded in 1862. Ladurée became famous for inventing the modern double-decker macaron and grew into a global symbol of luxury and elegance.

Following that example, our group started baking its own pastries and named them Pâtisseries Plain. Today we serve famous cakes, pies and beverages, each one made to feel like a small celebration.
